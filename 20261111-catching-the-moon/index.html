撈月

Catching the Moon

A wordless shadow play about an old fisherman who wants the moon.
一齣沒有台詞的皮影戲：一個想要月亮的老漁夫。

About the film · 關於這支片（看完再打開）

Nothing here was filmed, and no image or video model was used. Every frame comes from a small shadow-theatre renderer written for this film. It treats each leather puppet as a coloured filter at some distance from the paper screen and lights it from an oil lamp behind. Puppets pressed against the paper cast crisp, coloured shadows. Anything closer to the flame casts a bigger, softer one.

The story runs on that optics. A real moth sits on the screen from the first frame. When it flies back toward the lamp, its shadow becomes a monster. When it lands on the paper again, it is smaller than the fisherman's hat. The puppeteer's hand works the same way: a sharp hand near the screen, then a vast shadow as it pulls back to pinch out the flame. In the last act the light comes from the moon through a round lattice window. Moonlight is a parallel light, so shadows stop growing, and the window's light waxes like the moon itself until the old man is standing inside it.

The score is synthesised in code: guzheng-like plucks, woodblocks and a bowed tremolo, timed to the same beat sheet as the picture. The flutter you hear follows the moth's real distance from the lamp. The gongs, a cymbal and the window are public-domain and CC0 recordings from Wikimedia Commons.

畫面不是拍的，也沒有用任何影像生成模型。每一格都由這部片專門寫的皮影戲台渲染器算出來：每片皮影都是離紙幕有一段距離的彩色濾片，背後點著一盞油燈。貼在紙上的影子清楚、有顏色；越靠近燈火，影子就越大、越模糊。

故事就靠這個光學原理。從第一格開始，紙幕上就停著一隻真的飛蛾。牠飛回燈邊時，影子變成怪物；牠再落回紙上，比漁夫的斗笠還小。操偶人的手也是一樣：靠近紙幕時是一隻清楚的手，退回去捏熄燈火時變成吞掉整個畫面的大影子。最後一幕，光從圓形花窗外的月亮照進來。月光是平行光，影子不再變大，窗光像月亮一樣由缺到圓，最後把老漁夫整個圈在裡面。

配樂也是程式合成的：像古箏的撥弦、木魚和拉弦的顫音，跟畫面用同一張節拍表。拍翅聲跟著飛蛾離燈的真實距離變化。鑼、鈸和開窗聲取自 Wikimedia Commons 的公有領域與 CC0 錄音。
