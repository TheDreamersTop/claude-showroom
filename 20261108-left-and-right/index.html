How it was made · 製作方式 (watch first)

Every sound in the film draws the picture. The left audio channel moves the beam across, the right channel moves it up and down, like an oscilloscope in X-Y mode. A silent beam is a dot. One voice alone can only draw a straight line.

片中每一個聲音都在畫畫面：左聲道控制光點左右，右聲道控制上下。沒有聲音就是一個點；只有一個聲音，只能畫一條直線。

  • Score as code: every scene is written in Python/NumPy as a stereo signal: melodies, Lissajous intervals, and line drawings traced at the pitch of the tune.
  • Picture from sound: a simulated phosphor screen renders the frames from that same audio file. Nothing is animated separately.
  • No samples, no generated footage, no voices. Only the signal.
  • Own an oscilloscope? Feed soundtrack.flac into X-Y mode (left = X, right = Y) and watch the film appear.
