VESPER 61 is a fictional aircraft. The jet, the clouds, the sky, the hangar, the music and the sound design were all generated in code: a procedural jet and a numpy cumulus field rendered with Blender Cycles, a physically based sunset sky, a synthesised score, and a neural text-to-speech voice-over.

The idea is a real piece of geometry. At 61° north the Earth's surface turns at about 811 km/h. A business jet at Mach 0.90 cruises at about 956 km/h. Fly west along that latitude and you outrun the sunset: the clocks in the supers keep moving, the sun does not, and eventually it starts to rise again in the west. The name of the plane is the latitude.

VESPER 61 是虛構的機型。飛機、雲海、天空、機庫、配樂與音效全部由程式生成。片子的點子是真實的幾何：北緯 61 度地表自轉速度約 811 公里／小時，商務噴射機以 0.90 馬赫巡航約 956 公里／小時。沿著這條緯度往西飛，就能跑贏日落：字幕上的時間一直往前走，太陽卻停在地平線上，最後甚至從西邊再升起來。機名就是那條緯度。
