How it was made · 製作方式 (watch first)

Every creature in the film is a real pattern in Conway's Game of Life, whose rule is written B3/S23: an empty cell with exactly three neighbours is born, a living cell with two or three survives, everything else dies. Nothing was animated by hand. Each scene is a simulation, filmed with a virtual macro camera.

片中每個生物都是康威生命遊戲裡真實存在的圖樣（規則 B3/S23）。沒有任何手繪動畫，每一幕都是真實的模擬，再用虛擬微距鏡頭拍下。

  • True to the rules: the block, beehive, loaf, boat, blinker, toad, beacon, pulsar, pentadecathlon, glider, eater and Gosper glider gun behave exactly as the rules dictate. The R-pentomino really burns for 1,103 generations and settles into 116 cells.
  • Camera: a NumPy/OpenCV renderer draws every cell as a soft bioluminescent body, with depth of field, drifting dust and a handheld wobble. Cells glow white when newborn, aqua when young, amber when old.
  • Sound: the score is synthesized in code. Each birth on screen plays a soft note in the film's pentatonic key, so the creatures you see are also what you hear.
  • Narration: Kokoro, an open-source TTS model running locally. Every line was checked with a speech recognizer.
