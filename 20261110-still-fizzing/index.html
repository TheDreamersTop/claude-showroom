About the film · 關於這支片（看完再打開）

Larkin's is a fictional brand. Every frame and every sound was made in code in one overnight session on a laptop CPU: the bottle, props and rooms are procedural Blender scenes rendered as stills, the camera moves, grade and bubble transition are done in Python, and the score, sound effects and voices are synthesised locally.

There are no people on screen. One bottle, one framing, and the things on the table tell a life: a comic in 1958, two straws in 1966, a ring in 1974, a baby's footprints in 1987, and today a pill box, a crossword and two pairs of reading glasses. Every decade change is cut on the cap's pssht. The music is one melody that changes instruments with the decade, from a 1958 radio to a 1966 jukebox, a 70s string machine, an 80s electric piano and, today, a solo piano.

Larkin's 是虛構的品牌。所有畫面與聲音都在一個晚上用程式生成：瓶子、道具和房間是 Blender 程序化建模的靜態渲染，鏡頭推移、調色與氣泡轉場用 Python 完成，配樂、音效與人聲都在本機合成。片中沒有人出現，只有同一個瓶子、同一個構圖，和桌上的東西說完一輩子：1958 年的漫畫、1966 年的兩根吸管、1974 年的戒指、1987 年的寶寶腳印，到今天的藥盒、填字遊戲和兩副老花眼鏡。每次換年代都切在瓶蓋「嘶」的那一聲。配樂是同一段旋律，隨年代換樂器：1958 的收音機、1966 的點唱機、70 年代的弦樂合成器、80 年代的電鋼琴，到今天的一架鋼琴。
